# Start here
## What this doc is
This doc is the blueprint for the GSCG group ticket website for the Houston Astros. Everything that will be on the website lives here first: every page, every section, every word, image and link.
We build it together, section by section. Each section has an owner. When the doc is done, Mateo builds the final website from it, so if it is not in this doc, it will not be on the site.
The goal: any group, from a 20-person youth team to a 7,000-person school district, can land on the site, see what a day at Daikin Park looks like, understand the price, pick a date and start booking in a few minutes.
## What the website must include
Every page and section below should help with at least one of these. They come from P.J.'s feedback and our project scope.
- Visuals: people can see what a group day at the park looks like.
- Contact info: who to call or email with questions.
- Tiers and pricing: the six game tiers, clearly different from each other.
- Seating: where groups sit and what each area is good for.
- Schedule: available dates, connected to our theme nights and ideas.
- Interest form: a clear way to say "we are interested, let's get the ball rolling." This is separate from contact info.
- Built for every kind of group, not just our four. Our ideas (theme days, fundraising, Spanish-language outreach) show up as options anyone can choose.
- The value is obvious: in the first few seconds a group should see why this is worth it for them.
- One clear button to start booking on every page.
## Rules for everything we write
- No made-up facts, prices, dates or stats. If we do not know it yet, write "To confirm".
- Every stat has a source link next to it.
- No Astros logos or official branding. The site is a GSCG concept and says so.
- Write for the group leader: short, clear, friendly. No jargon.
- Images: an online photo with the credit and link, or an AI image labeled "Concept image".
## How this doc is organized
- Heading 1 is a page of the website (Home page, Experiences page, and so on). Pages show up in the website menu in the order they appear here.
- Heading 2 is a section on that page. Sections appear on the page top to bottom in the order they appear here.
- Under every section, use these labels: Owner, Status, What goes here, Layout idea, Content, Images, Links.
- "What goes here" explains the job of the section. Do not delete it. Write your work under "Content".
- Open the outline on the left side of the doc (View, then Show outline) to jump between pages and sections.
## How to change things
- Change words: edit anything under "Content" in your sections. For someone else's section, switch to Suggesting mode (pencil icon, top right) or leave a comment.
- Move a section: cut the Heading 2 and everything under it, then paste it where you want it. The order here is the order on the page.
- Add a section: type a new line, set it to Heading 2 (Format, Paragraph styles), then copy the labels from any other section under it.
- Add a page: same as a section, but use Heading 1, then add its sections with Heading 2.
- Remove a section: do not delete it. Set Status to Cut and add a comment saying why. Mateo removes it at the end of the week.
- Disagree or have an idea for someone else's section: leave a comment and tag them with @ and their name.
## Status key
- Idea: just a thought, not written yet.
- Draft: written, still being worked on.
- Needs info: waiting on a fact, a price, a photo or P.J.
- Ready: final, checked, sources added.
- Cut: we are not using it.
